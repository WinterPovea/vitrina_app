## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-touch, sensory, and serene aesthetic tailored for bespoke nail couture and contemporary aesthetics salons. The emotional tone balances boutique luxury with approachable warmth—evoking clean linens, polished lacquer, powdered mineral pigments, and calm personal pampering. 

The aesthetic marries **Refined Editorial Minimalism** with **Warm Tactile Translucency**. Interfaces feature expansive ivory whitespace, micro-hairline borders tinted in warm taupe, and subtle satin glassmorphic overlays for floating interactive controls. Imagery takes center stage: macro photography of nail artistry, botanical care oils, and luminous skin finishes. Avoid harsh mechanical drop shadows, flat utilitarian gray lines, and loud commercial pop-art motifs.

## Colors

The color palette reflects artisanal craftsmanship, luxury skincare, and high-fashion lacquer collections:

- **Primary (`#6D1A36`) — Deep Burgundy / Ripe Raspberry:** An assertive, decadent jewel tone used for primary call-to-actions, active booking states, high-priority highlights, and authoritative typographic headers.
- **Secondary (`#E8C8C4`) — Powder Rose / Bare Silk:** A soft, cosmetic pink applied to subtle chip backgrounds, active pill highlights, badge tints, and tonal section dividers.
- **Tertiary (`#C9A86A`) — Champagne Silk / Vintage Gold:** A metallic accent reserved for customer loyalty tier indicators, star ratings, premium treatment ribbons, and delicate icon trims.
- **Neutral Base (`#FAF6F3`) — Warm Linen Nude:** The foundational canvas tone, providing warmth that bright stark white cannot convey.
- **Neutral Foreground (`#261E1D`) — Espresso Noir:** An ultra-deep roasted taupe/black for primary text, offering softer contrast than pure `#000000`.
- **Neutral Muted (`#8C7E7A`) — Cashmere Taupe:** Used for secondary labels, metadata, durations, and inactive icons.
- **Success / Direct Booking Action (`#2E5D4B`) — Forest Jade:** Reserved strictly for instant WhatsApp and confirmed calendar statuses, harmonizing naturally with blush tones.

## Typography

The typographic tension pairs the editorial, high-fashion cadence of **Bodoni Moda** with the ultra-legible, geometric gentleness of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Bodoni Moda** is strictly deployed for prominent headlines, salon naming, treatment category hero titles, and celebratory booking confirmations. Its high stroke contrast exudes runway elegance and artisanal beauty.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** governs functional hierarchy: service durations, specialist biographies, pricing matrices, calendar pickers, and interface buttons.
- Capitalize small badges and treatment badges using `caption-caps` to evoke perfume bottle and luxury cosmetic packaging typography.

## Layout & Spacing

The mobile layout operates on a flexible 4-column system anchored by a `1.25rem` (20px) outer edge margin to ensure tap targets avoid bezel crowding on modern edge-to-edge smartphones. 

- **Vertical Rhythm:** Service cards, specialist carousels, and time slot sections utilize `space-xl` between sections to ensure a breezy, spa-like experience that avoids claustrophobic interface crowding.
- **Sticky Actions:** The primary booking bar and instant contact floating drawer sit docked at the bottom with a 34px bottom safe-area offset and internal padding of `space-md`.
- **Specialist & Lookbook Carousels:** Enable overflow snapping with edge-bleed to the screen border while respecting the `margin` indent on the lead item.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy abandons heavy synthetic drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Warmth, Satin Glassmorphism, and Low-Contrast Golden Borders**:

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Flat base `#FAF6F3` (Linen Nude).
- **Level 1 (Cards & Service Sheets):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface accompanied by a soft, diffused ambient blush shadow: `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(109, 26, 54, 0.04)`, bordered by a 1px hairline border of `rgba(232, 200, 196, 0.45)`.
- **Level 2 (Floating Specialist Badges & Quick Action Dock):** Frosted glass panel with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, surface fill `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82)`, and subtle shadow `0px 12px 32px -6px rgba(38, 30, 29, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Bottom Sheets & Salon Image Zoom):** Elevated white container framed by `0px 20px 48px -8px rgba(109, 26, 54, 0.14)` with a darkened backdrop tint in `rgba(38, 30, 29, 0.4)`.

## Shapes

The form language is rounded and tactile (`roundedness: 2`), reflecting the organic contours of sculpted almond nails, cosmetic compacts, and polished river stones:

- **Cards & Sheets:** Master radius set to `1rem` (16px) for service containers, specialist previews, and calendar tiles. Bottom sheets utilize an exaggerated top radius of `1.5rem` (24px).
- **Interactive Buttons & Chips:** Handcrafted pill format (`9999px` border radius) on all primary call-to-actions, category tags, and duration selectors to make them soft and inviting to touch.
- **Specialist Avatars:** Perfect circles (`50%` radius) or tall organic arch masks (`border-radius: 40px 40px 16px 16px`) for portfolio imagery.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Appointment CTA:** Deep burgundy `#6D1A36` background, crisp white `#FAF6F3` text, pill shape, `48px` minimum height, subtle active state scale `0.98`.
- **Secondary (Direct Contact - WhatsApp / Phone):** Dual split button. Left partition (WhatsApp) uses soft sage-cream background (`#F0F6F3`) with emerald text (`#2E5D4B`) and icon. Right partition (Direct Call) uses nude cream (`#F3EBE7`) with deep espresso text (`#261E1D`).
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** No background, hairline underline in `#C9A86A`, `Plus Jakarta Sans` medium text for secondary navigation like "Ver todos los servicios".

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Category Filter (e.g., 'Acrílicas', 'Nail Art', 'Spa Pedicure'):** Inactive chips use a `#FFFFFF` surface with `1px` border of `rgba(232, 200, 196, 0.6)` and `#8C7E7A` text. Active chips invert to `#6D1A36` with crisp white text.
- **Time Slot Selectors:** Pill buttons showing time (e.g., `16:30 hrs`). Disabled slots have strikethrough styling in `#D8CFCB`. Selected slots display a delicate ring of `#C9A86A`.

### Specialist Cards
- **Structure:** Horizontal or vertical rounded card containing:
  - High-res portrait framed in a softened arched frame.
  - Specialist name (`title-md` in `#261E1D`) and specialty (e.g., "Master Nail Artist & Russian Manicure").
  - Gold star rating badge (`#C9A86A` star icon + `4.9 (124)`).
  - "Próximo turno hoy" mini-tag in soft blush (`#E8C8C4` fill with `#6D1A36` text).

### Service Cards & Booking Sheets
- Compact list card displaying service name in `title-md`, duration badge (e.g., "75 min" with a miniature clock icon), price prominently displayed in `headline-sm` burgundy, accompanied by a small `+ Agregar` pill action.
- Micro gallery thumbnails of past nail works directly expandable from within the card.

### Inputs & Calendar Selectors
- **Date Carousel:** Horizontal strip showing day abbreviations (caps) and numeric dates. The selected day turns into a filled burgundy circle with a tiny gold dot underneath.
- **Form Fields (Name, Phone, Notes):** Flat sand background `#F5EEEB` with an animated floating label, transitioning to a `1px` border in `#6D1A36` on focus.
